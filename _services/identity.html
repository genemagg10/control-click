Centralized identity management is the foundation of secure, efficient operations. We help organizations streamline how users are provisioned, managed, and offboarded across Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 environments, ensuring the right people have the right access at the right time.

Common obstacles we resolve:

  • Former employees still having access to company systems
  • Inconsistent group memberships that don't reflect actual roles
  • Manual, error-prone user provisioning processes
  • Multiple passwords and logins across different applications
  • Lack of visibility into who has access to what
  • Compliance concerns around access controls and audit trails

Capabilities include:

  • Directory cleanup, organization, and guardrails
  • SSO integrations for third-party applications
  • Automated workflows for joiners, movers, and leavers
  • Role-based access control design and implementation
  • Security policy enforcement and monitoring

Possible outcomes:

  • Reduced security risk from orphaned accounts and access drift
  • Faster, more reliable employee onboarding and offboarding
  • Single sign-on convenience across your application ecosystem
  • Clear audit trails for compliance requirements
  • Less IT overhead from manual account management
