Technology should empower you, not intimidate you. We offer patient, personalized training sessions to help you master your devices and apps at your own pace. Whether you want to video call family, organize photos, or simply feel more confident with your computer, we meet you where you are and teach in plain language.

Common obstacles we resolve:

  • Feeling overwhelmed or intimidated by technology
  • Not knowing how to use features on devices you already own
  • Difficulty staying connected with family through video calls
  • Photos scattered everywhere with no organization system
  • Concerns about online safety and scams
  • Learning new apps required for work or personal life

What we help with:

  • One-on-one training sessions at your pace
  • App and software tutorials tailored to your needs
  • Online safety, privacy, and scam awareness
  • Photo and file organization systems
  • Video calling apps like FaceTime, Zoom, and Google Meet
  • Written notes and guides you can reference later

Possible outcomes:

  • Confidence using your devices independently
  • Ability to video chat with family and friends
  • Better protection against online scams and threats
  • Organized photos you can actually find and share
  • Skills to learn new technology on your own
  • Reduced anxiety around technology
