We build reliable, secure small to mid-scale networks that actually make sense to maintain. Whether you're expanding offices, connecting remote sites, or cleaning up a spaghetti-wired legacy setup, we design networks that are fast, organized, and clearly documented.

Ubiquiti UniFi and Cisco Meraki are our go-to ecosystems for scalable, cost-effective deployments. We handle everything from VLAN segmentation and QoS tuning to site-to-site VPNs and guest network isolation, all with proper monitoring and alerting so you're never flying blind.

Common obstacles we resolve:

  • Slow or unreliable Wi-Fi coverage with dead zones
  • Networks that have grown organically without documentation
  • Security concerns from flat, unsegmented network designs
  • Difficulty connecting multiple locations or remote workers
  • Bandwidth bottlenecks affecting business-critical applications
  • Lack of visibility into network performance and issues

Capabilities include:

  • LAN/WAN architecture, design, and upgrades
  • Mesh and point-to-point wireless links
  • Firewall rules, segmentation, and intrusion protection
  • Traffic shaping, QoS, and bandwidth management
  • Observability, uptime monitoring, and automated alerts
  • Network documentation and clean hand-offs for internal teams

Possible outcomes:

  • Consistent, fast connectivity across your entire workspace
  • Clear documentation that any IT professional can follow
  • Reduced security risk through proper network segmentation
  • Proactive alerts before small issues become outages
  • Lower long-term costs through efficient, maintainable design
