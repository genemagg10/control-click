Your smartphone can do so much more than make calls and send texts. We help you set up, customize, and learn to use your iPhone or Android with confidence, whether you're getting a new phone or want to get more from the one you have.

Common obstacles we resolve:

  • New phone setup feeling overwhelming
  • Difficulty transferring contacts, photos, and data from an old phone
  • Not knowing which apps to download or how to use them
  • Text and icons too small to read comfortably
  • Concerns about phone security and scam calls/texts
  • Photos taking up all your storage space

What we help with:

  • Initial phone setup and data transfer from your old device
  • App installation, organization, and recommendations
  • Camera settings and photo management
  • Accessibility features like larger text, voice control, and hearing aids
  • Security settings, password managers, and safe practices
  • Connecting to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth devices, and smart home equipment

Possible outcomes:

  • A phone set up exactly how you want it
  • All your contacts, photos, and important data transferred safely
  • Confidence using your phone's key features
  • Better protection against scams and security threats
  • Easier-to-read display and more comfortable usage
  • Skills to explore new features independently
