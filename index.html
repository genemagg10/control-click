Questions People Actually Ask

The honest answers to the things you might be wondering but hesitant to ask. Here are a few — the rest are on the full FAQ page.

Are you going to replace my team with AI?

No — and we’ll put it in writing. We use AI to take the repetitive work off the people you have, so they can do the parts that need a human. We measure success by what your team can do, not by making it smaller. If someone pitches AI as a way to cut people, that isn’t us.

Is AI going to replace me?

No. You have more to offer than any tool. We use AI to take the busywork off your plate so you can spend time on the work you know and care about. It works for you. It does not take your place.

What will this cost?

Every package comes with a fixed price, agreed before we start — no meters running, no surprise invoices. The first 30-minute chat is free, and you’ll leave it knowing your options and rough costs either way.

What if my team hates new tools?

That’s usually a sign the tool didn’t fit how they work, or nobody showed them why it helps. We choose tools around your actual workflows and train people hands-on. If something isn’t getting used, we swap it or remove it — adoption is the whole point.

I’m not very technical. Is that a problem?

It’s exactly who this is for. We explain things in plain English, we go at your pace, and we leave you with notes you can look back at. You should leave more capable than you arrived.

Are you going to judge my messy setup?

Never. We’ve seen everything, and none of it is embarrassing to us — it’s just where you are today. No question is too basic, and nobody gets made to feel dumb. Ever.
